How Multi-Media LSB Steganography Works

01

Binary Encoding

Your secret message is converted to UTF-8 binary bitstrings with a secure termination delimiter attached.

02

LSB Substitution

The Least Significant Bit of each sample/pixel is replaced with a payload bit. The difference is mathematically tiny.

03

Lossless Preservation

Saved in lossless container formats (PNG, WAV, FFV1 AVI) so lossy codecs never compress away your hidden data.

04

High-Speed Recovery

Our extraction algorithms rapidly stream bits and instantly reconstruct the original text upon delimiter discovery.
